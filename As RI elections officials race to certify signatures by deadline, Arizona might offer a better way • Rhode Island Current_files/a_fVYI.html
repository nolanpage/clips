Independents running for governor in ‘do-or-die’ race to collect signatures

Nine independents have only 10 days to clear the 1,000-signature hurdle to qualify for the ballot in Rhode Island’s Sept. 9 general election. They must turn in nomination forms to local canvassing boards on Friday, July 10.
